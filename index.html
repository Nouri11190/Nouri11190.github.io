01 / ABOUT

A connected body of technology work, built one useful system at a time.

Mark Allen Group brings together practical work across AI infrastructure, software, developer tooling, data research, blockchain systems, and digital products.

The approach is simple: identify a real problem, understand the constraints, build a workable system, test it with evidence, document what happened, and improve what already works.

Projects are treated according to their actual stage of development. Experiments remain experiments until evidence shows that they are ready for something more.

05 / WORKING PRINCIPLES

Build with evidence.
Improve without losing
what already works.

01

Inspect before changing.
Understand the real system and its constraints before remediation.

02

Preserve useful work.
Prefer reversible, evidence-based changes over blind rebuilds.

03

Separate client and server.
Keep mobile clients lightweight and move heavy workloads to appropriate infrastructure.

04

Show proof, not hype.
Document what was tested, what failed, what changed, and what works now.
